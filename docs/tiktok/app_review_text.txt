Bikas Home Publisher is the internal publishing tool of the Bikas Home store (household goods, Kazakhstan). The store owner connects the store's TikTok account via Login Kit on our website.
- user.info.basic, user.info.profile: show which account is connected (name, avatar, profile link).
- user.info.stats: show follower, like and video counts to track the account's growth.
- video.list: list the account's own videos with views, likes, comments and shares to compare which videos perform best.
- video.upload: upload a finished video to the TikTok inbox as a draft, so the owner can edit it in the TikTok app and post it.
- video.publish: after the owner approves a video in our tool, post it directly to the owner's account with the caption and privacy level the owner chooses.
Only the store's own videos are posted, and nothing is posted without the owner's explicit approval.
